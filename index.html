04 / THEORY

Components and Their Functions

Light Source: Provides monochromatic light for the experiment.

Convex Lens: Helps produce a parallel beam of light.

Polarizer: Converts ordinary light into plane-polarized light.

Half-Shade Device: Divides the field of view into two halves for brightness comparison.

Solution Tube: Contains the optically active cane sugar solution.

Analyzer: Helps determine the angle of rotation.

Telescope: Used to observe the field of view.
